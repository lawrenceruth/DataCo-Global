## Recommendation page

![Recommendation](assets/Recommendation_Page.PNG)

**Insights**
- Sales of **$36.78M** produced **$3.97M profit (10.8% margin)** from 384,079 units and 20,652 customers.
- **Fishing earns $756,220.76 (19.1% of profit).** The top three categories earn about 42%, and the top eight about 85%.
- **55.2% of units** in the biggest Cleats and Women's Apparel cells (Central America, Western Europe) are High Risk deliveries, at an unchanged margin of about 11.7%.
- YTD delivery time is 4 days against 3 previously.

**Recommendations**
1. Focus stock, marketing and service on Fishing, Cleats and Camping & Hiking.
2. Launch a delivery-reliability review for Central America and Western Europe.
3. Rationalise six tail categories that earn about 5.8% of profit combined.

## Executive Dashboard

![Dashboard](assets/DashBoard4.PNG)

**Insights**
1. **Sales are flat, profit is up.** Sales are $3.69M (-2.0% against $3.77M), while profit is $405.94K (+3.2% against $0.39M). Margin improved to about 11.0% from about 10.3%. Units are 29K (-3.8% against 30K).
2. **Segment profit differs from segment sales.** Consumer is 51.53% of sales ($1.90M) but 48.18% of profit ($195.59K), a 10.3% margin. Corporate ($1.13M, 30.65%) earns 11.8% and Home Office ($0.66M, 17.82%) 11.7%.
3. **Fishing leads profit by region.** Across the five regions Fishing earns about $150K, Cleats about $100K, Cardio Equipment about $82K and Water Sports about $67K. Garden is marginal.
4. **Delivery is stable but not improving.** Average delivery is 3 days, with the previous period and YTD both at 4 days.
5. **Context.** This view covers about 10% of company sales ($3.69M of $36.78M).

**Recommendations**
1. Review Consumer discounting in these regions to close the 1.5-point margin gap to Corporate (about $28K on $1.90M).
2. Keep Fishing and Cleats stock secure in the five regions, since together they earn about $250K.
3. Reset this page filter to All regions, product category, customer segment and date before executing action, so headline figures matches company total

## Sales Performance

![Sales](assets/Sales_Report.PNG)

**Insights**
1. **Fan Shop dominates.** Fan Shop is roughly $2.0M of $3.69M (about 54%). Apparel is about $0.9M, Footwear about $0.75M and Outdoors about $0.07M.
2. **Regional examples.** Central America Corporate reaches $481,757.36 and South of USA Consumer $227,999.51.
3. **Calendar-month pattern.** Pooled months peak at $0.73M in March, with $0.5M in February and $0.51M in April and May. They fall to $0.08M in August and September and recover to $0.26M in November.
4. **Delivery risk.** High Risk deliveries average 4 days against 3 for Low Risk.

**Recommendations**
1. Reduce dependence on Fan Shop by building Apparel and Footwear, which together are about 45% of sales.
2. Plan inventory and carrier capacity for the February to May window, which carries about 61% of pooled sales ($2.25M).
3. Reset this page filter to All regions, product category, customer segment and date before executing action, so headline figures matches company total.

## Customer Analysis

![Customer Analysis](assets/Customer_Report.PNG)

**Insights**
1. **Customer base.** This page shows 6K customers (against 15K previously), $3.69M sales, $405.94K profit and 29K units. The customer change does not reconcile and needs validation.
2. **Segment profit.** Consumer earns $195.59K (48.18%), Corporate $133.46K (32.88%) and Home Office $76.9K (18.94%). Corporate and Home Office both out-earn their sales share.
3. **Category volume.** Cleats leads units at about 14.8K of 29K (about 51%): 7.6K Consumer, 4.5K Corporate, 2.7K Home Office. Cardio Equipment follows with about 7.6K, and Fishing and Water Sports sell about 1.8K and 1.6K to Consumers.
4. **Market mix.** The largest market block is 7.7K units for Consumer (LATAM per the legend order), 4.9K for Corporate and 2.9K for Home Office.

**Recommendations**
1. Prioritise Corporate and Home Office growth in these regions, since both earn above-average margin.
2. Bundle Cleats with Cardio Equipment and Fishing to lift basket size.
3. Reset this page filter to All regions, product category, customer segment and date before executing action, so headline figures matches company total.

## Recommendation page (detailed)

![Recommendation](assets/Recommendation_Page.PNG)

**Insights**
1. **Scale and unit economics.** $36.78M sales, $3.97M profit, 384,079 units and 20,652 customers give $95.78 revenue and $10.33 profit per unit, and $1,781 sales, $192 profit and 18.6 units per customer.
2. **Growth quality.** Sales +43.3%, profit +44.6% and units +33.6% against $25.67M, $2.74M and 287K. Revenue per unit rose about 7%, so growth is partly price and mix.
3. **Category concentration.** Fishing $0.76M, Cleats $0.49M, Camping & Hiking $0.43M, Cardio $0.38M, Women's Apparel $0.35M, Water Sports $0.33M, Indoor/Outdoor $0.32M and Men's Footwear $0.31M earn about 85%. Computers, Electronics, Garden, Cameras, Children's Clothing and Crafts earn about $0.23M (5.8%).
4. **Largest cells.** Cleats in Central America (High Risk): $205,586 sales, $21,681 profit, 3,427 units, 902 customers. Cleats in Western Europe (High Risk): $193,408 sales, $25,663 profit (13.3% margin), 3,204 units, 850 customers.
5. **Delivery exposure.** High Risk is 55.2% of units across four complete cells ($692,344 sales at 11.7% margin), against Low Risk at $562,486 and 11.9%.
6. **YTD signal.** January 2018 YTD is $331.65K sales, $33.84K profit (10.2% margin) and 4-day delivery.

**Recommendations**
1. Concentrate commercial effort on the top three categories (about 42% of profit).
2. Fund a carrier and ship-mode review in Central America and Western Europe, targeting the 55% High Risk share.
3. Rationalise the tail categories and review Shop By Sport ($0.13M).
4. Segment the 20,652 customers into value cohorts using the $192 average profit as the baseline.
